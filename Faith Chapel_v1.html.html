About Us

Our Story

Founded in 1985, Faith Chapel exists to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We are a multi-generational family marked by grace and truth.

What We Believe

We believe in the Bible, the power of prayer, and serving our community. Our doctrine is centered on Christ’s love and the Holy Spirit’s guidance.
