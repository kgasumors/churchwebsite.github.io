Our Story

25 Years of Faith, Community & Purpose

Our Foundation

Built on the Word,
Rooted in Love

Grace Fellowship Church was founded in 1999 by Pastor Emmanuel and Ruth Adeyemi with a simple vision: to create a church where every person could encounter the living God and find genuine community. What began as a small gathering of 30 believers in a community hall has grown into a thriving congregation of over 1,200 members.

We are a non-denominational, Spirit-filled church that holds firmly to the authority of Scripture while embracing every believer with the grace and love of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To be a transforming presence in our city and beyond — a church that reflects the Kingdom of God where the broken are made whole, the lost are found, and every life is transformed by the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ through authentic worship, Spirit-filled preaching, intentional community, and sacrificial service — equipping every believer to live out their faith in every area of life.

What We Stand For

Core Values

01
Scripture First

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and the ultimate authority for faith and practice in our lives and community.

02
Radical Welcome

We believe every person bears the image of God and deserves to be welcomed with dignity, love, and respect — regardless of background or history.

03
Intentional Discipleship

We are committed to helping every member grow from spiritual infancy to maturity through ongoing teaching, mentorship, and accountability.

04
Generous Service

We express our faith through action — serving our city, supporting the vulnerable, and partnering with global missions.
